# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, and deliberate disruption are not
acceptable. Discuss technical decisions on their merits, assume good faith,
and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when necessary to protect a healthy project environment.
Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
